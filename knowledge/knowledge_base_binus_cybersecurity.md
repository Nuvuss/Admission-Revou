# KNOWLEDGE BASE: PROGRAM APPLIED CYBERSECURITY WITH AI (BINUS x GREATNUSA x REVOU)

## 1. IKHTISAR PROGRAM
- **Nama Program:** Applied Cybersecurity with AI (Threat Detection, Defense, and Security Operations)
- **Kolaborasi Institusi:** BINUS University, GreatNusa, dan RevoU.
- **Dukungan Infrastruktur:** AWS-Powered Labs (Amazon Web Services).
- **Durasi & Format:** 12 Minggu Hybrid (Live Online Sessions + 5x Sesi On-Campus di Kampus BINUS University).
- **Sertifikasi:** *Professional Certificate of Achievement* diterbitkan secara bersama oleh BINUS University, AWS, dan RevoU melalui kelulusan tatap muka di kampus BINUS.

---

## 2. JADWAL & METODE BELAJAR
- **Jadwal Kelas Online:** Senin, Selasa, & Kamis (19.30 - 21.30 WIB) - *Lecture & Simulation*.
- **Komponen Pembelajaran:**
  - 24 Live Lectures + 10 Hands-on Workshops.
  - 3 AWS-built Live Cloud Infrastructure Labs.
  - 5 Artifact Portofolio (1 artifact per modul).
  - 5 Sesi On-Campus di BINUS University (Networking, simulasi, dan defenses/graduation).

---

## 3. DOKUMEN & MATERI KURIKULUM (12 MINGGU)

### MONTH 1: Build Strong Foundations
- **Week 1: Thinking Like a Security Practitioner**
  - Orientasi, teori ke kapabilitas praktis, insiden keamanan & dampak bisnis.
- **Week 2: Building a Security Capability Map**
  - Framework kapabilitas keamanan, prioritas & trade-offs, pemikiran dari sudut pandang penyerang, desain Security Capability Map (Simulasi).
- **Week 3: Understanding How Attackers Behave**
  - Security operations praktis, interpretasi alert, Applied Threat Triage (Simulasi).
- **Week 4: Sorting Real Threats From Noise**
  - Analisis sinyal vs noise, metodologi threat triage, Threat Triage Workshop (**Hybrid Session AWS Lab 1**).

### MONTH 2: Strengthen Technical Skills
- **Week 5: Responding to Incidents & Communicating Clearly**
  - Siklus Incident Response, pelaporan teknis insiden, simulasi From Detection to Incident.
- **Week 6: Network Security: Architecture, Protocols & Defensive Controls**
  - Arsitektur jaringan & keamanan protokol, pemetaan ancaman jaringan & pemilihan kontrol (**Hybrid Session AWS Lab 2**).
- **Week 7: How Applications Get Compromised**
  - Lanskap keamanan aplikasi, kerentanan aplikasi umum, pemetaan attack surface aplikasi (Simulasi).
- **Week 8: Analyzing Vulnerabilities Like a Practitioner**
  - Diagnostics keamanan praktisi, Vulnerability Assessment, interpretasi Laporan Disclosures (Simulasi).

### MONTH 3: Apply & Demonstrate
- **Week 9: Fixing the Problem: Defensive Controls & Working With Developers**
  - Defensive controls, komunikasi keamanan, pembuatan Prioritised Remediation Brief (Simulasi).
- **Week 10: Autonomous AI in Security Operations**
  - AI otonom dalam SOC, konfigurasi AI Security Agents, pengembangan Capstone, Autonomous AI Assessment (**Hybrid Session AWS Lab 3**).
- **Week 11 - 12: Professional Readiness & Capstone Defense**
  - Bimbingan Capstone Project, persiapan presentasi & mentoring, *Professional Positioning*, Defense Portofolio & Kelulusan (**Hybrid Session On-Campus BINUS**).

---

## 4. AWS LABS & SIMULASI PRAKTIS
1. **Lab 01 - Threat Triage Simulation:** Investigasi queue alert realistis, identifikasi ancaman nyata, dan latihan prioritisasi insiden layaknya SOC Analyst.
2. **Lab 02 - Network Security Simulation:** Eksplorasi lingkungan AWS untuk memetakan ancaman jaringan, menganalisis kerentanan, dan memperkuat strategi defensif.
3. **Lab 03 - Autonomous AI Assessment:** Konfigurasi, deploy, dan evaluasi *AI-powered security agent* untuk mendukung operasional keamanan modern.

---

## 5. CAPSTONE PROJECT & PORTOFOLIO DELIVERABLES
- **Fokus Project:** *AI-Assisted Security Tooling & Automation* menggunakan workflow modern *vibe coding* untuk menyelesaikan tantangan operasional keamanan nyata.
- **Deliverables:**
  - Working Security Tool (Tools keamanan yang berfungsi penuh).
  - Walkthrough Arsitektur & Workflow.
  - Bukti Pengujian Fungsional & Eksekusi.
  - Justifikasi Keamanan & Operasional.
  - AI Use Log & Kalibrasi (Validasi output AI dan koreksi eror).

---

## 6. TARGET AUDIENS / PROFIL PESERTA
- **Students & Fresh Graduates:** Membangun fondasi praktis melalui simulasi skenario dunia nyata.
- **Early IT Professionals:** Memperkuat keahlian teknis dengan menerapkan AI-assisted workflow di lingkungan operasional.
- **Technical Professionals:** Memperluas kemampuan cyber defense untuk melindungi sistem dan menginvestigasi ancaman berbasis AI.
