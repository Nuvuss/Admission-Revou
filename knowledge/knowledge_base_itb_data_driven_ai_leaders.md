# KNOWLEDGE BASE: PROGRAM DATA DRIVEN & AI-ENABLED DECISION MAKING FOR LEADERS (REVOU x ITB)

## 1. IKHTISAR PROGRAM
- **Nama Program:** Data Driven & AI-Enabled Decision Making for Leaders
- **Kolaborasi Institusi:** RevoU x Institut Teknologi Bandung (Direktorat Pendidikan Profesional Berkelanjutan ITB).
- **Durasi & Format:** 12 Minggu Hybrid (Live Online Sessions + 2 Hari On-Campus Immersion di Kampus ITB Ganesha, Bandung).
- **Sertifikasi:** Sertifikat Resmi Completion & Digital Badge terakreditasi dari ITB (Institut Teknologi Bandung).
- **Sifat Program:** Executive Training (No Coding, No Complex Formulas) - Fokus pada *Decision Making*, *AI Deployment*, dan *Institutionalizing Data Culture*.

## 3. RINGKASAN STRUKTUR MODUL (12 MINGGU)

### PHASE 1: Decision Leadership Foundations (Week 1 - 4)
- **Week 1:** AI and Data-Driven Leadership Decisions (Decision Framing & Symptoms to Decision).
- **Week 2:** Fundamentals of Data Thinking for Leaders (Data Structure Literacy: Entities, Time, Granularity, Definitions).
- **Week 3:** Metrics and Evidence (KPI Design, Data Visualization Literacy, Decision Specification - Translating Decisions into Delegation-Ready Requirements).
- **Week 4:** Designing Experiments (Evidence Leaders Can Trust & Turning Results Into Decisions).

### PHASE 2: Leveraging AI for Decision Making (Week 5 - 8)
- **Week 5:** AI Systems for Leaders & Prompt Engineering (Designing a Custom AI Assistant for Decision Workflows).
- **Week 6:** Operationalizing Decision Workflows with AI-Enabled Automation (Creating Custom AI Workflows Simulation).
- **Week 7:** AI for Strategic Decision Making Framework & Analytics.
- **Week 8:** AI for Market Intelligence & Deep Research for Decision Validation (Building Business Case for AI Initiatives).

### PHASE 3: Institutionalizing AI-Governance & Case Study (Week 9 - 12)
- **Week 9:** Decision Operating System (Building Data-Driven Habits in Teams) & Executive Data Review Simulation (Interrogate, Trust, Decide).
- **Week 10:** Decision Economics (ROI of Data: Cost of Error, Cost of Delay, Value of Information) & Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) for AI.
- **Week 11:** Capstone Project Kick-Off (AI-Enabled Decision Challenge).
- **Week 12:** Capstone Delivery, Defense, & Graduation Session di Kampus ITB Ganesha.

---

## 4. CAPSTONE BUSINESS CHALLENGE & PLAYBOOK
Peserta tidak mengerjakan kasus hipotetis, melainkan membawa masalah keputusan nyata dari organisasi/perusahaan masing-masing. Output akhirnya berupa **Implementation Playbook** lengkap yang berisi:
1. Decision Framework.
2. Data Plan & Metrics.
3. AI Workflow & Automation Design.
4. Governance Model & Risk Mitigation.
5. Actionable Implementation Roadmap.
