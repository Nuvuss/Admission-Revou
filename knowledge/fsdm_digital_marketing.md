## 3. KURIKULUM LENGKAP (12 MODUL INTEGRASI AI)

### Module 1: Measuring Performance & Budget Allocations
* **Materi:** Marketing funnel metrics, Alignment Business Goals to Marketing KPIs, perhitungan CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) & CLTV (Customer Lifetime Value), alokasi budget, media planning.
* **Tools:** Google Sheets.

### Module 2: Product Marketing and Landing Pages
* **Materi:** Product-Market Fit, Value Proposition (VP), Unique Selling Proposition (USP), Landing Page Design & Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO).
* **Tools:** Unbounce / Leadpages.

### Module 3: GenAI Fundamentals (AI Module)
* **Materi:** Prompt engineering untuk digital marketer, pembuatan Custom GPTs untuk workflow pemasaran.
* **Tools:** ChatGPT.

### Module 4: Google Analytics, UTM, GTM
* **Materi:** GA4 Setup & Navigation, UTM tracking tags, Google Tag Manager (GTM), Pixel/Event tracking, Attribution Modeling.
* **Tools:** Google Analytics 4, GTM.

### Module 5: Basic Analytics, Reporting & Data Visualization
* **Materi:** Data Analysis, Measurement Plan, Dashboarding, Reporting dengan AI.
* **Tools:** Looker Studio, Google Sheets, AI Analytics Assistants.

### Module 6: Social Media Organic (AI-Integrated)
* **Materi:** Buyer Persona, Brand Identity, Instagram & TikTok Algorithm Optimization, Copywriting, Video Production, Social Media Audit.
* **Tools:** Meta, TikTok, Canva, CapCut.

### Module 7: Influencer Marketing (KOL)
* **Materi:** KOL sourcing & tiering, pembuatan brief, User-Generated Content (UGC) strategy, analisis ROI campaign KOL via UTM.

### Module 8: Meta Ads (AI-Integrated)
* **Materi:** Struktur & objektif Meta Ads, strategi kreatif, A/B testing, targeting audiens, analisis kompetitor, piksel tracking, optimasi AI untuk materi iklan.
* **Tools:** Meta Business Suite, AI Creative Tools.

### Module 9: TikTok Ads & Marketplace Ads
* **Materi:** TikTok Ads & Spark Ads, TikTok Pixel, Marketplace Strategy (Shopee & Tokopedia CPAS), Affiliate Marketing, Live Shopping.

### Module 10: Google Ads
* **Materi:** Search Ads, Keyword Research & Match Types, Conversion Tracking, Budgeting, Campaign Optimization, Performance Max & Demand Gen.
* **Tools:** Google Ads.

### Module 11: SEO with AI (100% AI-Powered)
* **Materi:** Topic & Keyword Research, On-Page & Technical SEO, Struktur Website, Prinsip E-E-A-T, AI for SEO content creation.
* **Tools:** Google Trends, Google Search Console, AI SEO Tools.

### Module 12: Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
* **Materi:** Multi-channel CRM (Email, WhatsApp), Customer Segmentation, Lifecycle Mapping, Campaign Automation, A/B Testing, Strategy Retention.
* **Tools:** Mailchimp, Whimsical.
