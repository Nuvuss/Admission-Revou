# PROGRAM 1: ITB – Data Driven & AI-Enabled Decision Making for Leaders

### 1. Positioning & Value Proposition
- Program 12 minggu untuk leaders dan managers yang ingin membuat keputusan lebih tajam, deploy AI di workflow kerja, dan menginstitusionalisasi budaya data-driven di organisasi.
- Tag Resmi: *Online and Live Sessions · ITB Ganesha Campus Immersion · ITB Certified*.
- Pesan Utama: Bottleneck adopsi AI di perusahaan jarang berada pada teknologi, melainkan pada **leadership readiness** (kesiapan pemimpin).
- Filosofi: Program ini **tidak mengajarkan cara kerja teknis AI**, melainkan mengubah cara leader dan timnya mengambil keputusan strategis dengan AI di setiap langkah.
- Key Pitch Hook: *"The biggest risk isn't adopting AI too fast. It's watching others do it while you're still waiting for certainty."*
- Pendaftaran: Review rolling basis, kuota kursi terbatas.

### 2. Program Summary
- **Institusi Mitra:** RevoU × Institut Teknologi Bandung (Direktorat Pendidikan Profesional Berkelanjutan ITB)
- **Durasi:** 12 Minggu
- **Learning Mode:** Hybrid (Live online sessions + 2 on-campus days di Kampus ITB Ganesha, Bandung)
- **Jadwal:** Selasa & Kamis, 19.30–21.30 WIB
- **Kredensial:** ITB Certificate of Completion + Digital Badge resmi ITB

### 3. What's Included
- 12 minggu live interactive sessions (Selasa & Kamis, 19.30–21.30 WIB)
- 2 sesi on-campus di ITB Ganesha Campus (networking eksekutif dan seremoni graduation)
- Latihan hands-on dengan AI tools nyata (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, n8n), diterapkan ke konteks kerja peserta
- Capstone project individu + implementation playbook dengan expert panel defense

### 4. 4 Pembeda Utama ("Not Another AI Course, but an Institutional Transformation")
1. **Built for the person making the call:** Dirancang untuk leader yang perlu bertanya dan bertindak atas analisis. *No coding, no complex formulas*.
2. **Backed by a century of ITB credibility:** ITB berdiri sejak 1920 (#1 technical university di Indonesia). Peserta mendapat sertifikat resmi ITB dan jaringan alumninya.
3. **AI embedded throughout:** AI diperkenalkan sejak sesi pertama dan diperdalam di ketiga fase (bukan modul tempelan).
4. **Capstone = Masalah bisnis nyata:** Setiap leader membawa decision challenge nyata dari organisasinya dan pulang membawa implementation playbook siap eksekusi.

### 5. Program Outcomes
- **Scale AI across your organization:** AI governance framework, operating rhythm tim berbasis data, menciptakan kondisi agar AI tertanam di organisasi.
- **Measure AI ROI with confidence:** Menentukan metrik yang mencerminkan business driver nyata (bukan sekadar vanity metrics), membangun infrastruktur performance tracking.
- **Walk away with an executable playbook:** Decision framework, AI workflow, tata kelola data (governance model), dan roadmap implementasi.

### 6. Struktur Modul Mingguan (12 Minggu)
- **Phase 1: Decision Leadership Foundations (Week 1–4)**
  - Week 1: AI and Data-Driven Leadership Decisions; Decision Framing (from symptoms to decision).
  - Week 2: Fundamentals of Data Thinking for Leaders; Data Structure Literacy (entities, time, granularity, definitions).
  - Week 3: Metrics and Evidence (KPI design + data visualization literacy); Decision Specification (translating decisions into delegation-ready requirements).
  - Week 4: Designing Experiments (evidence leaders can trust); Turning Results Into Decisions.
- **Phase 2: Leveraging AI for Decision Making (Week 5–8)**
  - Week 5: AI Systems for Leaders & Prompt Engineering; Designing a Custom AI Assistant for Decision Workflows.
  - Week 6: Operationalizing Decision Workflows with AI-Enabled Automation; Creating Your Own Custom AI Workflow (simulation).
  - Week 7: AI for Strategic Decision Making Framework; AI for Analytics & Data-Driven Decision.
  - Week 8: AI for Market Intelligence & Deep Research for Decision Validation; Building the Business Case for AI Initiatives.
- **Phase 3: Institutionalizing AI, Governance & Case Study (Week 9–12)**
  - Week 9: Decision Operating System (building data-driven & AI-enabled decision habits in teams); Executive Data Review Simulation (interrogate, trust, and decide).
  - Week 10: Decision Economics / ROI of Data (cost of error, cost of delay, value of information); Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) for Data and AI Strategy.
  - Week 11: Capstone Project Kick-Off (AI-Enabled Decision Challenge).
  - Week 12: Capstone Project Delivery & Submission; Graduation Session di Kampus ITB Ganesha Bandung.

  - Week 4: Hypothesis testing, effect size, A/B experimentation; Process variation, SQC, experiment monitoring.

  - Week 9: Reproducibility, testing, Git, production-ready analysis; Model monitoring, drift detection.
  - Week 10: Agentic analytics, workflow automation; Konversi model ke data product (Streamlit interface + decision memo).
- **Module 4 & Capstone: Defend (Week 11–13):**
  - Week 11: Capstone build sprint (2 clinics + red-team review bersama mentor).
